Play for Salone

We are building Sierra Leone's national football programme from the ground up — and we are looking for players, coaches, officials and volunteers, at home and across the diaspora.

Anyone aged 15 or over with a connection to Sierra Leone — by birth, through a parent or grandparent, or otherwise — wherever in the world you live. You do not need to have played American football before. Athletes from football, athletics, basketball and rugby backgrounds are all part of how the game grows here.

If you are under 18, we will ask for a parent or guardian's details before you can take part.

Who can register

Anyone aged 15 or over with a connection to Sierra Leone — by birth, through a parent or grandparent, or otherwise — wherever in the world you live. You do not need to have played American football before. Athletes from football, athletics, basketball and rugby backgrounds are all part of how the game grows here.

If you are under 18, we will ask for a parent or guardian's details before you can take part.

What happens next

Registrations go onto SLAAF's athlete database, which is how we select for combines, training sessions and, in time, national squads. We will be in touch by email — so please use an address you check.
