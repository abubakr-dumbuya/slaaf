About SLAAF

The Sierra Leone Authority of American Football is the sole governing authority for American football — flag and tackle — in Sierra Leone. We are a non-profit, non-political sports federation recognised under the laws of Sierra Leone.

Our mission

To develop, promote and govern American football in Sierra Leone as a tool for youth empowerment, education, health, leadership and national pride.

Our vision

To build a sustainable, inclusive and internationally respected American football ecosystem that creates opportunities from grassroots to elite levels.

What we exist to do

  1. 01Govern and regulate American football nationwide.
  2. 02Develop grassroots, school, university, community and elite pathways.
  3. 03Promote gender equity, inclusion of persons with disabilities, and youth participation.
  4. 04Train, license and certify coaches, referees, officials and administrators.
  5. 05Organise national leagues, competitions, clinics and development programmes.
  6. 06Represent Sierra Leone in regional and international American football bodies.
  7. 07Safeguard athlete welfare, integrity and ethical conduct.
  8. 08Build strategic partnerships to advance the sport.

What guides us

  • Integrity and fair play
  • Transparency and accountability
  • Inclusion and equal opportunity
  • Athlete-centred development
  • Democratic governance
  • Compliance with national and international sports standards

How we are governed

SLAAF is governed by a Constitution adopted by its General Assembly and most recently amended on 28 January 2026. Executive Board members serve four-year terms and are elected in free, fair and transparent elections. Amendments to the Constitution require a two-thirds majority of the General Assembly.

The federation operates in compliance with the National Sports Authority, the Ministry of Sports, and applicable international federation statutes. Where national law conflicts with our own rules, national law prevails.

General Assembly
The supreme authority of SLAAF. It elects the Executive Board, approves budgets and audited accounts, and amends the Constitution. It meets annually.
Executive Board
Governs SLAAF between General Assemblies and is accountable to the Assembly. It meets at least quarterly and decides by majority vote.
Standing Committees
Technical & Coaching, Development & Grassroots, and Events & Competitions.
Advisory Board
Provides counsel to the Executive Board on strategy and the development of the sport.
Judiciary Committee
An independent body responsible for discipline and dispute resolution.
Athletes' Commission
Represents the interests and welfare of athletes within the federation.
